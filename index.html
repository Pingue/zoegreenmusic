Classical Guitar

I am a passionate classical guitarist and believe it is the most beautiful and colourful instrument with a stunning repertoire to explore. I make the most of the adaptability of the guitar to teach a wide range of music: for me it is a tool in which to teach an understanding of music, as well as the techniques of playing the guitar.

I achieved Grade 8 guitar in 2008 and continued to study for a degree in Music at the University of Birmingham where I majored in performance.

Music Theory

I offer bespoke music theory lessons for those approaching the ABRSM or Trinity theory exams or to enhance GCSE or A-level music courses. My approach to theory is with sound, bringing it to life to unpick how music works and combining it with playing and composing. I believe in pupils having a stronger theoretical knowledge to understand and enjoy the music they play more deeply, rather than learning it as an abstract concept.

My experience as a classroom music teacher gives me a multitude of ways to explain and explore theoretical aspects of music.

I achieved Grade 8 theory of music in 2018 on the Trinity board as well as holding a degree in Music from The University of Birmingham.

Safeguarding

It is of vital importance that children feel safe in my lessons at all times. To ensure this, I have the following in place:

DBS
I have an enhanced DBS certificate on the update service which can be viewed at any time.
Annual training
I hold a part-time position with Ealing Music Service which requires me to complete safeguarding training annually in September after KCSIE (Keeping Children Safe in Education) updates. My annual safeguarding certificate is available to view on request.
Vigilance
My years of experience as a teacher give me the experience to be highly vigilant. I promise that anything disclosed to me by the child during a music lesson will be reported back to the parent or to the local authority designated officer (LADO), depending on the nature of the disclosure.
No touch
I have a ‘no touch’ policy in music lessons. If I need to touch a child, for example to adjust their technique when playing guitar, I always say ‘Is it ok if I touch your hand?’ before doing so, and it is always fine for pupils to say no.
Transparency
Parents are welcome to sit in on lessons whenever they wish to. I am a parent to a child who also has music lessons and therefore also hold a parents’ perspective. You are welcome to raise any issues at any time.
Other adults
Access to my garden music studio can be made without going through my house. However, for peace of mind my husband also holds a valid DBS certificate on the update service.
